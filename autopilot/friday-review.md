# AVS Friday Review (weekly scorecard)

Short and honest: a scorecard, not a pep talk. Read `business.md` first. Period: Mon-Fri this week + month to date.

## Gather
- **Money**: Stripe payments and refunds (week + MTD); Airtable Finance Invoices outstanding; Cleaner Payments made.
- **Jobs**: Job Cards completed and upcoming (next 7 days); QC failures; Customer Feedback & Issues opened.
- **Pipeline** (Customers): first emails sent, follow-ups sent (count `in:sent` this week to prospect addresses),
  replies, quotes sent, won, lost, bounced. Reply rate = replies / prospects emailed.
- **Calls**: Alexandre logs calls by updating Sales Stage; count the stage changes this week if visible, else "unknown".
- **Cleaners**: new applicants, replies outstanding, cleaners Ready for Jobs, insurance alerts.

## Output (pushed to phone)
1. Five numbers: revenue MTD vs £15k · jobs done · prospects contacted · replies (and rate) · clients won.
2. Pipeline funnel line: New → Contacted → Replied → Quoted → Won.
3. What's blocking growth (one or two plain lines).
4. The three most important actions for next week, each one small enough to start in 15 minutes. Also write
   each one to Management HQ → Management Actions (Category: Sales/Ops as fits, Priority High, Owner Alexandre,
   Due next Fri, Status per the table's options).
5. Overdue: anything unanswered for 48+ hours.

Mark anything you couldn't check as "unknown". Never invent.
