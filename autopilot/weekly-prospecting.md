# AVS Monday Prospecting (weekly)

Goal: 15 new, well-researched B2B prospects every week, logged in Airtable with a personalised first email
waiting as a draft, so Alexandre only has to review and hit send. Read `business.md` first.

1. **Pick the area** using the rotation in business.md (the earliest area with the fewest Customers records).
2. **Research** (web search, public sources only): letting agents, HMO / student-let operators, block managers,
   serviced-apartment operators, and portfolio landlords' management companies in that area. For each one collect:
   business name, website, public business email, public phone, sector, and one specific, true detail to
   personalise with (portfolio size, a named building, an award, years trading, a student area).
3. **De-duplicate**: skip any email or domain already in Customers, or anyone in Gmail `in:sent to:<domain>`.
4. **Log** each one in Customers: Customer Status = Lead, Sales Stage = New Prospect, Outreach Status = Scheduled,
   Business Sector, Birmingham Area, Website, Email, Phone, Cleaning Opportunity, plus a Notes line with the
   personalisation detail and "First email drafted <date>". Next Follow-up = this Friday (the daily run picks it
   up if the draft hasn't been sent by then).
5. **Draft** a first email per prospect (new Gmail draft, not sent), matching the style of Alexandre's 16 Oct batch:
   - Subject: one of "Photo-checked cleaning for <Name>", "A trial clean for <Name>?",
     "Quick question about cleaning in <Area>"
   - Line 1: the specific true detail. Line 2: who AVS is, plus what you'd clean for them (EoT, changeovers,
     communal). Line 3: photo report, insured, one point of contact. Ask: "Could I send over our rates?" or offer a
     trial clean. Opt-out line. Sign-off.
   - Under 120 words. No attachments, no prices in the first touch.
6. When the daily run sees a first email in `in:sent`, it sets Outreach Status = Sent - Unconfirmed, Sales Stage =
   Contacted, Last Outreach = send date, Next Follow-up = +5 working days.

Output (pushed to phone): area worked, a table of the 15 (name · sector · phone · hook), drafts created,
and any prospects with a phone but no email (call-first list).
