"We already have a cleaner."

"Makes sense, most agents we speak to do. Would it help to have us as backup for the busy check-out weeks? Your first clean comes with a full dated photo report, so you can compare."

Give the fixed price from the table, then: "That includes the inside of the oven and fridge, a dated photo report the same day, and a free re-clean within 48 hours if anything's missed."

"That's more than we pay."

"We're not the cheapest and we don't try to be. A failed check-out or a deposit dispute costs more than the difference. The photo report is your evidence, and the re-clean is on us."

"Understood. When a tenant leaves it in a state, or you've got a quick turnaround, would it help to have someone you can book same week? Can I send our rates so they're on file?"
